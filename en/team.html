Team

Who is behind OTSPI

OTSPI is a young initiative, currently driven by a small, volunteer founding team. We prefer to say so plainly: the credibility of a future trust service provider is built on engineering rigour and transparency, not on size.

What we are looking for

One person cannot hold all the roles of a trust service provider: the standards require strict segregation of duties. The team therefore has to grow before any application for qualification. We are looking in particular for:

  • Authority OfficersHolders of root key cards and operators of key ceremonies, under a 3-of-5 quorum.
  • Information Security Officer (CISO)Leading the security policy, in a role independent of operations.
  • Trust Policy Committee membersExperts in cryptography, PKI and eIDAS compliance, independent of management.
  • Independent internal auditorChecking compliance with procedures, separate from operations.
  • Advisory boardFigures from public administration, research, audit and open source.
