Its draft statutes have not yet been put to the vote of the founding general meeting; the association has therefore not yet been declared and does not have legal personality.

Pending its formation, the website is published on a non-professional basis by Philippe Vienne, founding member of the initiative, who is also its publication director. In accordance with Article 6, III, 2 of French Law No. 2004-575 of 21 June 2004 on confidence in the digital economy, the publisher's personal identification details have been provided to the hosting provider named below, from which they may be obtained under the conditions laid down by law.

  • Publisher contact: contact@otspi.org

Once formed and declared, the association will become the publisher of the website and this notice will be updated.

Personal data

Data controller: Philippe Vienne, founding member of the OTSPI initiative, until the association is formed and takes over this role. Contact: contact@otspi.org.

This website sets no cookies and loads no resources from advertising, font or external statistics services. Its audience is measured with Matomo, as described below. Messages sent to contact@otspi.org are used only to reply to them and to follow up on exchanges with the OTSPI initiative. You have the right to access, rectify, erase, object to and restrict the processing of your data, which you can exercise at the same address. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL).

Audience measurement

To understand how the site is used (pages viewed, approximate origin), the publisher uses Matomo, configured to be exempt from consent under CNIL guidance: no cookie or identifier is set, IP addresses are truncated, only aggregated statistics are produced, the “Do Not Track” signal is honoured, and the data are neither combined with other processing nor shared with third parties for other purposes. The instance is hosted by o2switch (France), under the publisher's control. Raw data are kept for 180 days; aggregated statistics for at most 25 months. Legal basis: the publisher's legitimate interest in measuring the audience of its site.

You may object to this measurement on this page or by writing to contact@otspi.org.

Manifesto signatures

Signatures of the manifesto are collected through a form hosted by o2switch (France) and operated by the publisher. The data collected are first name, last name, e-mail address and, optionally, position and organisation.

  • Purposes: recording signatures, verifying them by a confirmation e-mail and publishing the list of signatories who have expressly consented to it.
  • Legal basis: consent, which may be withdrawn at any time.
  • E-mail addresses are never published. Names, positions and organisations are only published with the person's explicit consent.
  • Retention period: an unconfirmed request is deleted within 7 days; signatures are kept for the duration of the campaign, then no more than two years after it closes; the public list is kept for as long as the manifesto is online, unless withdrawn.
  • Withdrawing a signature: link in the confirmation e-mail, which deletes the signature and the data. Exercising your other rights: contact@otspi.org.
  • The form sets no cookies; IP addresses are only kept as a salted hash for one hour, to limit abuse.
